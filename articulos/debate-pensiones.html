The Debate · Opinion, not news

If living 150 years were possible, who would pay the pensions?

November is National Family Caregivers Month in the U.S. AARP research published on October 28 estimates that family caregivers spend $11,508 a year out of pocket on average (almost 60% more than in 2021); with dementia, $15,846. Caregiving consumes on average 31% of household income. The science being tested today in 3 people does not change that bill tomorrow — but if it matures, its first beneficiaries will not be only the patients, but those who care for them.

Question for readers: what should change first, the science or support for those who care?
