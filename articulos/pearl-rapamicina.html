Human trial · Primary goal not met

A year of weekly rapamycin was relatively safe, but missed its primary goal

The PEARL trial (Moel et al., Aging, 4 Apr 2025) enrolled 129 adults and 114 completed 48 weeks of weekly rapamycin. Its primary goal — reducing visceral fat measured by DXA — did not change (p = 0.942). Only lean mass and pain improved, as secondary findings, in women on 10 mg. That paper did not measure epigenetic clocks: the DunedinPACE and GrimAge2 figures circulating from an aggregator do not hold up and are not published in this edition. It is a story of scientific honesty, not a breakthrough.

Sources
