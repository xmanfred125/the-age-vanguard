First person treated with a senolytic immunotherapy: a peptide that teaches the immune system to clear “zombie” cells

Immorta Bio announced on November 2 the first human administration of SenoMune™-0257, a nine-amino-acid peptide, in a patient with advanced cancer treated on October 29, 2026 in Mexico (Integrative Cancer Centers of America). The company says safety and efficacy in humans are not established. It is a first step, not a result: no efficacy data have been published.

None of these therapies can be bought or received outside a trial.
