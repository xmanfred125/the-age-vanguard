In the lab and in animals · No human data

The DNA-repair switch that still works in old cells

On November 1, in Nature Communications, Joana C. Macedo and Elsa Logarinho’s team reported that boosting the protein KIF2C — repressed in aged cells — improves repair of double-strand DNA breaks, the most dangerous damage. A small molecule (UMK57) restored repair in aged cells and in progeria and Down syndrome cells, and prolonged life in progeria mice.

Caution

Progeria is a model of aging, not normal aging; there are no human data and no treatment.

Sources

Macedo et al., Nature Communications, 1 Nov 2026, via paisanoai.substack.com.
