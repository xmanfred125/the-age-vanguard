At 80, her first marathon: she started walking at 77

Mildred Baker, from the Bronx, joined a walking class for older adults in the New York Road Runners Striders program at 77. Three years later, on Sunday, November 1, 2026, she ran her first full New York City Marathon, at 80, in 7:03:49. She had finished her first half marathon in 2025, at 79. CBS profiled her training on October 31; she was one of the 26 runners on Team Inspire 2026, chosen for their personal stories. Fewer than 500 runners aged 80 or older have finished this marathon in its 55-year history. Her method: walk 2 minutes, jog 2, walk 1. It is not a recipe: it is proof that starting late still counts.
