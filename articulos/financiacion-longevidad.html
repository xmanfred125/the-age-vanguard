Longevity money: more than $3 billion in 62 rounds in 2026

Data updated November 5, 2026.

Longevity fundraising is growing: 62 deals worth at least $3 billion so far in 2026, versus 46 deals and at least $2.4 billion in the same period of 2025, according to the NewMarketPitch tracker. The largest round was Neko Health’s $700 million Series C (July 2026); 11 rounds above $50 million total $2.5 billion. By company, Altos Labs has raised $2.6 billion in total, NewLimit $760 million after its $435 million Series C in June 2026, and BioAge Labs more than $492 million. The uncomfortable fact: the median round fell from $16 million to $10 million, and money excluding the big rounds fell slightly.

What happened

The sector already exceeds the whole of 2025 (at least $2.8 billion) before the year closes.

Why it matters

Capital validates longevity as an industry, but it concentrates in a few big names; most small startups raise less.

What comes next

Traction over the next 2–3 years will depend less on round sizes than on regulatory precedents, because the FDA does not recognize aging as a treatable disease.

Caution

These are figures from a commercial tracker, not audited data; they should be checked against a second tracker before being cited as definitive. SECONDARY SOURCE.
