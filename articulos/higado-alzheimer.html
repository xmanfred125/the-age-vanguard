The liver as an Alzheimer’s filter: in old rats, reopening it halved blood amyloid protein

Researchers at the Keck School of Medicine (USC, Journal of Biological Chemistry, November 2026) showed that the liver clears beta-amyloid from the blood through cells with pores that close with age. Two weeks of the experimental drug cinaciguat in aged rats reopened the pores and blood beta-amyloid fell by half, to the levels of young rats.

Caution

It is not an Alzheimer’s treatment or a result in humans.
