Clearing “zombie” cells improved liver fibrosis in almost half of those treated

A randomized phase 2 trial (Amsterdam, published in Nature Metabolism in 2026) tested dasatinib + quercetin (D+Q) in 31 people with fibrotic MASH. After three 7-week cycles, 47% of those treated improved at least one fibrosis stage, versus 7% on placebo; MASH resolution was 53% versus 7%.

AI & Brain

In animals

The liver as an Alzheimer’s filter: in old rats, reopening it halved blood amyloid protein

Researchers at the Keck School of Medicine (USC, Journal of Biological Chemistry, November 2026) showed that the liver clears beta-amyloid from the blood through porous cells that close with age. Two weeks of the experimental drug cinaciguat in aged rats reopened the pores and blood beta-amyloid fell by half, to the levels of young rats. It is not an Alzheimer’s treatment or a result in humans.

Myths vs. Science

Myth: “Chemical cocktails that reverse cell age in a week already exist.”

Science: In 2023 a Harvard team showed in cultured cells that six chemical cocktails restored youthful markers within days. No animal treated with those cocktails lived longer, no human received them, and there is no commercial product delivering them at those concentrations. The related path that did reach people is the genetic one, ER-100 (see the lead story). Source: chuckfreshvoice.medium.com
